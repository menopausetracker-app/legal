# Privacy Policy — Menopause Tracker

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Menopause Tracker ("the app") is made and published on Google Play by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: menopausetracker.app@gmail.com.

## The short version
- Your health records stay on your phone. We never receive them.
- There is no account and there are no ads.
- Purchases go through Google Play. Our payment partner RevenueCat sees an anonymous ID and your purchase details, not your health data.
- Anonymous usage statistics are off unless you turn them on.

## Data stored on your phone
Everything you log — check-ins, hot flashes, bleeding, medications, doses, side effects, your profile answers and the optional name for the doctor report — is stored only in the app's storage on your device. We have no server that receives it.

This data leaves your phone only when you choose to:
- **PDF report.** When you share or print the report, it goes wherever you send it.
- **Backup file.** An exported backup is encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256). We can't open it.
- **Android backup.** If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account. This is controlled by your phone's settings and Google's terms, not by us.

## Purchases (RevenueCat and Google Play)
Payments are processed by Google Play. We never see your card details.
To check whether you have Premium, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). RevenueCat receives:
- a random anonymous ID created on your device,
- your purchase and subscription status (product, dates, renewal, store transaction ID),
- basic technical data (platform, OS and app version, store country).

This happens even if you never buy anything, so the app can tell you're on the free plan and restore purchases on a new phone. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Usage statistics (PostHog) — only if you turn them on
Menu → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage statistics" is **off** by default. If you turn it on, the app sends a short list of events to PostHog (EU servers, Germany): for example "app opened", "check-in completed", "report created", "paywall shown", "purchase completed", and once a day the number of yesterday's hot flashes as a range (0, 1–3, 4–9, 10+).

Events never include your symptoms, scores, bleeding, medication names, notes or dates. They carry a random anonymous ID and basic device data (device model, OS and app version, language, time zone). We've turned off IP address storage. You can turn statistics off at any time; sending stops immediately.

## What we don't do
- No advertising and no advertising ID.
- We don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.
- No location, contacts, photos or other files are accessed.

## Notifications
Daily reminders and the "trial ends in 2 days" reminder are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them.

## Deleting your data
- Menu → Delete all data removes everything stored by the app on your phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.
- To delete the anonymous IDs and records kept by RevenueCat or PostHog, email menopausetracker.app@gmail.com. Please include the app's purchase details if you have them; without an account we may need them to find your records.

## Children
The app is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
